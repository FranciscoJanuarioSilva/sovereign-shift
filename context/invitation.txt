Hackathon Portugal 2026

Hackathon Theme

The theme of this event will be “InfraHack: AI for Cloud Infrastructure”.

InfraHack explores how AI can be used to make cloud infrastructure smarter, safer, more efficient and more independent, with a focus on cloud operations, security, data protection, and migration.

AI for cloud infrastructure is the focus, and we want participants to demonstrate a creative mindset and ability to problem-solve in a collaborative environment.

We want to see a showcase of knowledge and understanding, with considerations including factors such as data protection, cloud security, and sovereign cloud.

Do you have an idea for hyperscaler alternatives, infrastructure automation, or more secure cloud operations? Show us what you’ve got!

The Evaluation Criteria

• Innovation: the judges will be looking at originality, creativity, and the relevance of solutions submitted.
• Applicability: consider real-world potential, ideally demonstrated through a prototype or demo.
• Technical complexity: quality will be taken into account, along with implementation and use of AI and cloud infrastructure.
• Presentation & clarity: there should be a demonstration of an ability to explain the problem, solution, technical approach, and impact.

Who Can Participate

This event is open to developers and tech professionals of all levels.

You can register for free as a team of 3 to 5 people or as an individual who will be assigned to a team. Note: you will need to bring your own laptop.
